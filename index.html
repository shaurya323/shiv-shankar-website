// About the works

Built to your drawing, tested before it leaves the shop.

We don't work off a fixed catalogue. Every panel, booth, conveyor, or oven starts from your site's load schedule and layout, gets fabricated and wired at our Hitkari Industrial Complex unit, and is checked and signed off by our technical director before it ships — so what arrives on site is what was ordered, not a close approximation.

We work directly with plant engineers and EPC contractors — no middlemen, no catalogue guesswork. Tell us the load, the space, and the site conditions, and we build to that, not to a standard SKU.

What we manufacture

Five product lines, each built to order for the site it's going into.

01 / PANELS

Electric Control Panels

Custom LT control panels built to your load schedule and site layout, fabricated and wired in-house.

DETAILS
Built to IS 8623 panel standards · CRCA sheet enclosures · copper busbar & MCCB/MCB protection · powder-coated finish · tested before dispatch.
02 / POWER FACTOR

Power Factor Control Panels

APFC panels sized to correct your plant's power factor and cut down on utility penalty charges.

DETAILS
Automatic Power Factor Correction with stepped capacitor banks · relay-based controller · sized against your monthly billing data.
03 / FINISHING

Painting Booths

Enclosed spray booths with filtered airflow for a clean, controlled paint finish on the shop floor.

DETAILS
Dry-filter or water-wash extraction · sized to your part dimensions · sized ducting & blower to suit booth volume.
04 / MATERIAL HANDLING

Conveyors

Belt and roller conveyor systems designed to your line speed and product handling requirements.

DETAILS
Belt, roller & slat conveyor configurations · geared motor drives · built to your line speed and load rating.
05 / THERMAL

Electrical & Diesel Fired Ovens

Industrial curing and drying ovens in electrical or diesel-fired configurations, built for continuous duty.

DETAILS
Insulated chamber construction · digital temperature control · electrical or diesel-fired heating, sized to batch or continuous duty.
